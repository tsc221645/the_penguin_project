# Editorial and scientific content policy

Species writing is original, concise, and intended for curious visitors. Important claims are stored with source IDs and review dates. Conservation status always includes an assessment year, source, and project review date.

The directory currently follows the 18 extant species convention represented by the IOC World Bird List reference. Taxonomy is a documented convention, not a permanent universal species count.

Missing measurements, diets, behavior, or breeding details remain unavailable. The project never fills gaps with estimates or copied text. Directory-only entries are visibly distinguished from completed profiles until their facts, sources, and imagery are reviewed.

Source roles are kept distinct:

- Wikidata may provide identifiers and taxonomy data.
- Wikimedia Commons provides individual image rights records.
- BirdLife, IUCN, NOAA, Smithsonian Open Access, GBIF, and peer-reviewed research may support scientific claims according to their terms.
- Wikipedia may help orient the directory, but it is not treated as the final authority for a conservation assessment or a reusable image.
